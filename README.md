## Project Title

**SpeakUp – A Free Guide to Public Speaking**

## Project Overview

Build a modern, responsive, educational website called **SpeakUp** that teaches beginners and school students the fundamentals of public speaking.

This is **NOT** an online academy, coaching institute, or paid course website.

Instead, it should feel like a passion project created by a student who wants to help other students gain confidence in speaking before an audience.

The website should feel friendly, motivational, informative, elegant and inspiring.

Design it similar to modern educational websites with beautiful animations, glassmorphism cards, smooth scrolling, gradients, illustrations, and dark/light mode.

Personal Inspiration & Educational Journey

Create a dedicated section titled "My Journey" that tells the authentic story behind the creation of SpeakUp. The writing should be warm, humble, and motivational, emphasizing that this website is a personal passion project rather than a professional academy.

Include the following story naturally:

I discovered my interest in public speaking after one of my school teachers noticed my clear pronunciation and accent.
My accent was not natural—it was developed through years of consistent practice.
Since 2021, I have made it a habit to read books aloud, including story books, school textbooks, encyclopedias, general knowledge books, and educational articles.
Reading aloud helped improve my pronunciation, fluency, vocabulary, confidence, and speaking rhythm.
My teacher encouraged me to participate in speeches, anchoring, assemblies, and competitions because they believed I had the potential to become a confident speaker.
Over time, I became recognized by teachers and students in my school as a capable speaker and anchor.
I received opportunities to represent my school in several competitions, including institutional competitions and inter-school CBSE events in Riyadh.
Although I have not yet won these competitions, every experience has taught me valuable lessons and made me a better speaker.
My goal is now to help beginners begin their own journey and realize that public speaking is a skill anyone can develop with dedication.
